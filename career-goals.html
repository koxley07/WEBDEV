Career Goals

What are my short term career goals?

These goals will help me strengthen my current technical capabilities while preparing for a transition into software development. I plan to attend more conferences in the EW realm to provide input and receive feedback on improvements to the systems and training that can be used to better help the Navy.

What are my long term career goals?

My long-term goal is to transition into software development while applying my experience in electronic warfare and Navy systems. I would like to join a software development team at NSWC Crane or contribute to large language model and machine learning development through the Electronic Warfare Directorate, supporting advancements within my current career field. I am also interested in developer opportunities with Naval Special Warfare Command or its supporting contractors. Another path I would like to explore is developing cloud-based applications and services using Amazon Web Services (AWS) or Microsoft Azure. Across these opportunities, I aim to combine my technical troubleshooting background with programming, artificial intelligence, and cloud development skills to build reliable solutions that support operational needs.
